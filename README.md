# serve 💅
### Stamp it. Slay it.

A fast-paced crafty chaos game where your only mission is to SERVE the perfect paper design before time runs out 💅

Match the target pattern by picking cute stamps and placing them on paper as quickly as possible. Hearts, stars, crowns, flowers — every thappa matters.

Cute? Yes. Stressful? Also yes.

Made for **Blo_xy's Crafty Jam** — Theme: *Crafty*

🎮 Play it here: https://sleepyme06.itch.io/serve

---

## 💖 How To Play

- Look at the target pattern
- Pick a stamp
- Place it on the paper
- Match everything before the timer hits zero

That's it. No lore. Just vibes and pressure.
